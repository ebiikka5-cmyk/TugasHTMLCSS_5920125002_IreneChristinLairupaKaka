Magnolia

Magnolia grandiflora.commonly known as the Southern magnolia of bull bay.is a the tree of the family Magnoliaceae native to the southeastern United States, from coastal Virginia South to central Florida , and west to eastern Texas and Oklahoma. Reaching 27,5 m (90 ft) in height, it is a large striking evergreen tree with large dark green leaves and large white fragrant Flowers.
