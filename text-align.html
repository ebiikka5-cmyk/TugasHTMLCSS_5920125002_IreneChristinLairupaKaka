Breed History

The briard, which is believed to have originated in Fance, has been bred for centuries to herd and to protect sheep. The breed was used by the French Army as sentries, messengers and to search for wounded soldiers because of its fine sense of hearing. Biards were used in the first World war almost to the point of extinction. Currently the population of briards is slowly recovering. Charlemagne, Napoleon, Thomas jrfferson and Lafayette all owned briards
